# Publication announcement

**Certified whole-domain bounds and equality barriers for the odd theta–Weil form** — Leonard van Hemert

This preprint gives explicit certified lower bounds across the full odd form domain of a theta-weighted Riemann–Weil form. The bounds retain positive energy while accounting for the complete residual, prime powers, pole terms and infinite tails. A quantitative equality barrier explains why the fixed positive comparison cannot reach zero loss through trial enrichment alone.

The release includes a concise paper, full supplement, reproducible finite certificates and Lean proofs of the central abstract argument and exact rational consequences. The theta–arithmetic inputs to those formal statements are identified explicitly. The result is a controlled odd-sector estimate; RH, zero-loss positivity, the even sector and the full Li transfer remain open.

Read and cite the preprint: [10.5281/zenodo.23337494](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23337494). Source and release assets: [LeonardSEO/odd-theta-weil-bounds](https://github.com/LeonardSEO/odd-theta-weil-bounds).
