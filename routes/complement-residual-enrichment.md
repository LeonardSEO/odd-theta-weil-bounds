## RE1 — The exact new columns and their operator domain

dyadic coefficients. Actual zero ordinates are the uniquely certified
ordinates in the owning native FLINT enclosures, not their binary
centers. Define

    r=B D1+V0 D2,       V0 e_j=w sin(gamma_j x),
    H=C*C,             P_F=C H^(-1) C*,
    U=(I-P_F)r.

The original physical frame theorem H>=h0 I, with
h0=(21/10^8)(1-10^-8)>0, proves this inverse and its meaning. Every
U-column is exactly in qperp intersect Fperp and D(E_Y).

Proof. For every fixed real gamma, W sin(gamma x) is Schwartz. The
complete theta representation, its jets and division by cosh give the
same fact for the existing W sin(t_i x), W sinh(x/2) and Phi' terms.
The complete bound b(t)<=17+9 log(1+|t|) makes b(D) applied to these
Schwartz functions L2. Multiplication by bounded w and the closed form
representation place all B and V0 columns in D(G)=D(E8). The score
q is already in that domain. Hence their score projections and finite
linear combinations are in D(E8). The finite H inverse gives another
finite linear combination of C, so P_F preserves the domain.
For v in D(E8) intersect qperp, E_Y v=S E8 v: test the full E8 form
against the quotient form domain. Thus all the asserted columns are
in D(E_Y). No high-zero-tail estimate is needed for this domain fact.

The exact entire old residual is R0=r-(E_Y-E_mid)Y0, Y0=CX_ET,
at the ET shift. ER4 bounds that difference because Y0 is in F. It
does not bound (E_Y-E_mid)U. The new primitive frequencies reach
2999.4944929945946, above the old actual-band maximum; even a single
new column can correlate with zeros just above the 3000 cutoff.

The numerical pilot uses the center projection

## RE3 — Exact Fourier data for the new full action

Let Omega(z)=FT(W)(z), Xi(z)=FT(Phi)(z), and
v(t)=integral W(x)sinh(x/2)sin(tx) dx, with positive Fourier convention.
For a column y=S y_base where

    y_base=w sum_a c_a sin(a x)+c_p w sinh(x/2),
    alpha=<q,y_base>/Q,

write FT(wy)(t)=i h_y(t). On real t this is exactly

    h_y(t)=sum_a c_a[Omega(t-a)-Omega(t+a)]/2
              +c_p v(t)+alpha t Xi(t).             (RE3)

All c_a,c_p are the actual B/V0 coefficients, including sqrt(mu_i^+),
and alpha uses the actual Q and score overlaps. The V0 score overlaps
are exactly zero at the actual ordinates. The pole overlap is -1/4.
The formula holds for complex coefficients as well. Binary output
coefficients are interpreted exactly; errors of sqrt masses, Fourier
values, zero locations and products remain finite certificate prices.

The full action is S w g_y, where

    g_y(x)=(1/2pi) integral ell8(t) i h_y(t) exp(-itx) dt.

Its energy Gram is the full Fourier integral
(2pi)^(-1) integral ell8(t) h_i(t)* h_j(t) dt. Its action Gram has
the two full frequency integrals with kernel Omega(t-s), followed by
the score subtraction. In particular a compressed energy Gram alone
does not certify the norm of the action or the whole residual.

## RE4 — A constructive rigorous Fourier-action enclosure

The following theorem pays for the whole L8 action, including both
physical tails and frequency tails. It is not a Riemann-sample
positivity argument.

For a finite trial map Y:C^m->D(E_Y) whose columns have the finite
RE3 theta/sine/pole/score representation, let H_Y(z)=FT(wY)(z), with
its m entries regarded as a row vector. This extra strip regularity
does not follow from membership in D(E_Y) alone. Take a=1/4 and
beta=1/8. The complete theta series proves

    |Omega(t+is)|, |FT(W sinh(x/2))(t+is)|, |Xi(t+is)|
        <=20 exp(-|t|/8),            |s|<=1/4.      (RE4a)

Here is an explicit whole-lattice proof of its constant. On the
horizontal physical lines |Im x|<=1/8, use 4pi²<40, 6pi<19,
pi cos(1/4)>5/2, and
|cosh((x+i theta)/2)|>=(1/2)cosh(x/2).
On x>=0, W times exp(x/4) is therefore bounded by

    sum_n [160n^4 exp(17x/4)+76n² exp(9x/4)]
                            exp(-(5/2)n² exp(2x)).

Set z=exp(2x). Bound the resulting fractional powers by z² and z.
Their integrals from 1 to infinity are bounded respectively by
(106/125)exp(-5n²/2) and (14/25)exp(-5n²/2), after dropping only
favorable n denominator powers. Since exp(-5/2)<1/10 and
n^4<=16^(n-1), the whole lattice sum is <=25/246. Including both
physical half-lines gives

    2[80(106/125)+38(14/25)](25/246)<20.

For W sinh use |sinh((x+i theta)/2)|<=cosh(x/2), so it is at most
twice Phi; its corresponding bound is smaller. Phi itself has a
smaller bound. Shift the physical contour by the signed 1/8 line,
retaining exp(|s||x|), to obtain RE4a. The superexponential theta
majorant kills both vertical ends. Thus every atom, both physical
tails and the complex frequency strip are retained.

The analytic continuation

    m_Gamma(z)=[psi(1/4+iz/2)+psi(1/4-iz/2)]/2-log(pi)

has no pole on |Im z|<=1/4. Its vertical derivative is bounded by 36:
the complete trigamma series there is at most
sum_(j>=0)(j+1/8)^(-2)<=64+8, and the two chain-rule factors give 36.
The existing b bound, |c_Gamma|<10, log(1+t)<=t, and the elementary
six-term bound |k8(z)|<=72 prove the deliberately generous bound

    |ell8(t+is)|<=200+10|t|,        |s|<=1/4.      (RE4b)

In RE3, put c_a,c_p and alpha as row vectors of trial coefficients.
An explicit bound for both strip-line L1 integrals of
L_Y(z)=ell8(z)H_Y(z) is

    A_Y=20[sum_a (4480+160|a|)||c_a||_2
                 +4480||c_p||_2+47200||alpha||_2]. (RE4c)

Indeed integral exp(-|t-a|/8)dt=16,
integral |t-a| exp(-|t-a|/8)dt=128, and
integral t² exp(-|t|/8)dt=2048. Apply RE4a–b to the two sine shifts,
the pole term and (|t|+1/4)Xi(t). The same A_Y bounds the real-line
L1 integral. Exact outward row norms give a valid A_Y; no observed
cancellation is required for this bound.

Choose spacing h>0, integer K, T=Kh and physical radius R>0. Define

    g_hat(x)=h/(2pi) sum_(|k|<=K) L_hat_k exp(-ikhx),
    A_hat_Y=S[w g_hat],

where each L_hat_k is an exact binary row chosen inside a certified
point enclosure. Define

    P=2pi/h,
    tail_h=h/(2pi) sum_(|k|>K)||L_Y(kh)||_2,
    node_h=h/(2pi) sum_(|k|<=K)||L_Y(kh)-L_hat_k||_2,
    B_h=h/(2pi) sum_(k in Z)||L_Y(kh)||_2,
    w_R=||w 1_(|x|>R)||_2.

All three sums have explicit finite upper prices. For the infinite
frequency tails, RE4a–b supplies shifted exponential envelopes. The
shifts retain the actual primitive frequency, not a presumed band
of the residual. Sums of t^j exp(-t/8), j=0,1,2, reduce to complete
geometric sums and their first two derivatives. The already accepted
stronger real envelopes 5 exp(-|t|/4) and 5|t|exp(-|t|/4) can be used
on the real tail. These tails contain every omitted grid ordinate.

Then the following is a whole-Hilbert-space action-map bound:

    ||S L8 Y-A_hat_Y||
      <=||w||_2 [A_Y/pi exp(aR) exp(-aP)/(1-exp(-aP))
                         +tail_h+node_h]
           +[A_Y/(2pi)+B_h] w_R.                  (RE4d)

Proof. Invert the Fourier transform on the signed strip lines.
For every real x, ||g_Y(x)||_row<=A_Y exp(-a|x|)/(2pi).
Poisson summation, justified by these analytic integrable bounds,
identifies the infinite trapezoid with
sum_(n in Z) g_Y(x+nP). On |x|<=R the omitted n!=0 terms have row
norm at most
A_Y exp(aR)exp(-aP)/[pi(1-exp(-aP))]. On |x|>R, bound the exact
inverse by A_Y/(2pi) and the infinite trapezoid by B_h. Multiply by
w and integrate on the two actual spatial sets. Deleting the
frequency tails and inserting the enclosed binary values costs
the two finite sums times ||w||_2. Finally S is contractive. Row
Euclidean bounds bound the operator norm of the trial map, proving
RE4d on its full output space, including outside the trial span.

## RE5 — Entire residual and matrix perturbation theorem

Let Y:C^56->D(E_Y) be any admissible exact trial with the same input
dimension as C, including Y=[C,U]X for an exact 112-by-56 selector X.
Assume A_hat approximates E_tilde Y with a genuine whole action-map
error <=e_gamma, including RE4d and any input/action approximation
prices. Put

    s=mu-rho56>0,   G=Y*Y,
    R_hat=C-A_hat-sY,
    K_hat=C*Y+Y*C-sG-(Y*A_hat+A_hat*Y)/2.

Every product here is a whole physical product. In particular the
action Gram A_hat*A_hat retains the component outside [C,U].
Finite numerical Gram errors must be propagated directly into these
matrices and their eventual Loewner upper endpoint. They do not
automatically constitute a whole-map error e_gamma; converting one
into such an error would require its own proved map estimate.
For arbitrary beta,tau>0, the exact resolvent obeys

    C*(E_Y+s)^(-1)C <= K_hat+epsilon_R G
                           +e_gamma||Y|| I
       +(1/s){(1+beta)R_hat*R_hat
             +(1+1/beta)[(1+tau)epsilon_R²G
                         +(1+1/tau)e_gamma² I]}.  (RE5)

Proof. Write E_Y Y=A_hat+Delta_R Y+D_gamma, with
||D_gamma||<=e_gamma. Bounded self-adjoint Delta_R gives the first
quadratic upper price epsilon_R G. The symmetric D_gamma quadratic
is at most e_gamma||Y|| I. For the entire residual use Young's
operator inequality twice, retaining both errors, then use the exact
ER5 shifted-resolvent identity. No projection of the residual, new
Gram inverse, operator coupling omission or spectral gap is used.

If a complete matrix certificate places (11/10) times the right
side below theta N, theta<1, it immediately proves

    a(u)>=(1-theta)E8(z)-[rho56+theta(mu-rho56)]||z||²

on the entire original odd form domain, with the already proved
closed verified kernel transfer z=(I-P_K)u. All original high-zero,
prime, contact and pole terms remain accounted for in that floor.
This theorem removes the old norm-only residual limitation while
retaining the actual outside-trial coupling in its action Gram.
